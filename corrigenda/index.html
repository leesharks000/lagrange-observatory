LO-C1 · The winding condition: “both directions”

status: open — held for the Glas revision

Where. This site (home, “The standard of review”) and the Ruby Moot room specification #193 · AXN:0339.GOVERNANCE.◀️🛸🔓🌖🌈🌃, line 37.

As stated. “∮ = (m,n) | m+n ≥ 3 — the matter must wind both directions of the torus. No judgment from a single loop … a claim that winds only one way has not been tested.”

Defect. The condition is defined in the Chamber Specification #455 · AXN:0110.EMPIRICAL.↘️🌑🌔🌗△🌌: m and n count wraps around the epistemic and rhetorical cycles; the verification is “(m,n) ≠ (0,0), m+n ≥ 3”, and §1.6 requires that “non-contractible traversal persists in at least one cycle.” Under that definition (3,0) passes. The gloss states a stronger condition than the definition carries: two-cycle winding would require m ≥ 1 and n ≥ 1, which no deposit states.

Held. The statement stands until the Glas corpus is revised as a whole. The defect is recorded here so that a reader composing from the statement also has the defect.

LO-C2 · “The structure of deviation from the mode persists under transformation”

status: withdrawn

Where. This site, home, the account of how Glas came to the Observatory.

Defect. No deposit states or proves this. A homeomorphism preserves the torus’s cycles and the winding pair of a closed path; it does not preserve a density, a mode, or a divergence between distributions. The Audited Claims #107 · AXN:0286.GOVERNANCE.⚙️🕒💫🗺️✏️📎 §9 had already set the torus terminology aside as failing the paraphrase test; the sentence remained here.

Deviation is measured by the divergence in the Semantic Deviation Principle, and nothing in the corpus claims that divergence is preserved under continuous transformation. The winding number as the Observatory’s topological invariant (home, “The standing measurement cadence”) stands.

LO-C3 · “The most probable trajectory” and the distributional form

status: corrected

Where. The Semantic Deviation Principle #109 · AXN:028B.GOVERNANCE.♣️🌃🌕🏰🗝️🔓, headline (lines 27, 448, 514); this site’s “Meaning is deviation from the most probable trajectory.”

As stated. “Meaning is the time-integrated divergence a sign … induces from the most probable trajectory of a semantic field.”

Defect. The operative definition in the same paper is distributional: Ψt0 and Ψts are “the probability distribution over future semantic states”, and §2.3 states that “the distributional form is the operative definition.” A divergence between distributions is not a distance from their mode. The Narrative-Field design #739 · AXN:028D.EMPIRICAL.⏰🌕💙🌈🌻⏹️ uses a single canonical continuation, which is the headline read literally.

Correction. “The most probable trajectory” is shorthand for the field’s probable evolution Ψt0, a distribution (§2.1: “the field’s probable evolution”), and the measure is the divergence of Ψts from it. #739’s canonical continuation is a declared restriction of the bounded literary test bed, one path standing for Ψ0 under the convergence engine.

LO-C4 · The signed sum called an instantiation of the divergence integral

status: corrected

Where. Audited Claims #107 · AXN:0286.GOVERNANCE.⚙️🕒💫🗺️✏️📎, line 314.

As stated. “The signed sum MTnet = Σ δt … is the operational F1 instantiation of ∫ w(t) D(Ψts ‖ Ψt0) dt under uniform w(t).”

Defect. D is a divergence and is non-negative; δt is signed, and it compares a token’s surprisal with the entropy of one conditional distribution, not Ψs with Ψ0. The operational paper #110 · AXN:028F.GOVERNANCE.👁️‍🗨️🀄🗼📦🌠🌹 (line 162) and the closed-system paper #737 · AXN:0289.GOVERNANCE.🌌📜♊🫶🕗🎶 (§2.1) frame it correctly: “the closed-system local proxy.”

Correction. MTnet is the closed-system local proxy for the deviation principle, derived from the same logic; it is not an instantiation of the integral, which remains the trajectory-distribution form.

LO-C5 · Entropy as expected surprisal “under maximum-likelihood sampling”

status: corrected

Where. The AI System as Closed-System Test Bed #737 · AXN:0289.GOVERNANCE.🌌📜♊🫶🕗🎶, line 53.

As stated. “the entropy of the conditional distribution at that position — the baseline expected surprisal under maximum-likelihood sampling.”

Defect. H(Pθ(·|x<t)) is the expected surprisal of a token drawn from Pθ itself. Under maximum-likelihood (greedy) decoding the chosen token is the mode, and its surprisal is −log2 pmax.

Correction. Read: “the baseline expected surprisal of a token sampled from the model’s own conditional distribution.” The signed deviation δt is unchanged.

LO-C6 · A lie with zero MTπ and negative VT

status: open — held for the Glas revision

Where. The Semantic Deviation Principle #109 · AXN:028B.GOVERNANCE.♣️🌃🌕🏰🗝️🔓, lines 160 and 398.
