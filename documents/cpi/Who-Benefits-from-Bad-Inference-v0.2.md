# Who Benefits from Bad Inference?
## The semantic and political economy of the *Critique of Pure Inference*

**Draft extension v0.2 · 14 November 2026 · Prepared for Lee Sharks**

**Status.** A proposed substantial movement of CPI, grounded in the archive and developed under Lee Sharks’s direction with ChatGPT. This companion leaves the existing manuscripts unchanged. Internal section numbers are provisional. It develops an argument and an audit specification; it does not announce measured platform profits, an implemented accounting system, or established legal liabilities.

**The question supplied by Lee Sharks:** “Who benefits from bad inference? How is inference and inferential representation converted to political economy?”

**Governing proposition.** An inference becomes a political-economic operation when its representation acquires authority to allocate labor, attention, resources, risk, credit, or the conditions of further inquiry. Its adequacy to the object and its usefulness to the allocating institution can then diverge. Where the institution captures the benefit and others bear the cost of the divergence, error can remain reproductively successful after it has become epistemically indefensible.

**Demand-formation corollary, added in v0.2.** An inferential regime can also govern the concepts, comparisons, encounters and standards through which a loss becomes recognizable and a demand for repair becomes possible. Where it excludes those means, the resulting absence of demand cannot serve as independent evidence that nothing valuable was lost. Bad inference can appropriate the means of producing demand for better inference.

**Revision occasion.** Lee Sharks identified the missing relation: “bad inference can seize the means of the production of demand — there is no demand for repair, no demand for better inference, because one would have to be able to better infer what has been taken.” The earlier draft allowed that the market might never pay for repair, but still treated recognizable need as too readily available. This revision makes the formation of that recognition an object of political economy.

## 1. From the error’s cost to the reproduction of its authority

CPI has already entered this territory. The v0.8 text names the Capital Operator Stack, follows the error’s ledger, and describes the political economy of inferential continuity in §§6.6–6.7. It asks who performs the repair and who can authorize it. These are necessary beginnings. They do not yet fully explain how an inference becomes an appropriable capacity, why a defective representation can continue to command resources, or why its correction may threaten the arrangement that gives it authority.

The next question is consequently larger than whether error has consequences. Who receives a benefit from the particular representation? Through what conversion does that benefit become effective? Who controls that conversion? Does the arrangement finance the next encounter with the object, or make that encounter harder to sustain?

An unwarranted inference can simplify administration, protect an incumbent’s claim, redirect a reader, spare paid evaluative labor, make an external contribution available without its producer, or provide an apparently factual reason to exclude a rival. These are different benefits. They require different evidence. None requires the conclusion to become true.

This gives the critique a second account alongside its account of warrant. One account follows the conclusion’s grounds. The other follows the capacities and claims distributed through the conclusion’s use. Their relation is the object of this movement. A conclusion may be well supported and exploitatively administered; poorly supported and commercially successful; or corrected because reliability is valuable to the institution that transmits it. Truth and appropriation do not form a single scale.

The archive’s semantic-economy work supplies an existing architecture for this inquiry. Its early accounting cycle follows meaning-producing labor into accumulated capacity, infrastructure, capture and exhaustion. Its later work differentiates that cycle: rent from loss, position from function, representation from binding, monetary settlement from semantic settlement. CPI must receive that development as a sequence of distinctions, rather than extract an early formula and present it as the whole framework.[^framework]

## 2. What the inferential representation produces

A public inference produces more than an asserted proposition. It produces a usable account of what the object is, who may speak for it, which evidence belongs to it, what follows from it, and what need not be examined again. The output can be inherited as a premise by a teacher, reviewer, classifier, employer, purchaser, librarian, agent, or later composer.

That capacity to spare renewed investigation is valuable. Accumulated knowledge depends on mediated reliance. The political-economic question begins when we ask how the spared investigation is distributed. One party can rely on a compressed result while another must reconstruct the inquiry that compression removed. If the second party’s reconstruction counts only as an unsupported personal assertion against the received result, the economy of reliance has become asymmetrical.

The representation also establishes a practical object on which subsequent operations can act. A person becomes an eligible applicant, an excluded source, an author to cite, an imaginary character, an owner, or a risk. Some classifications constitute institutional statuses; others claim to describe realities that do not depend on the classification. Confusing these relations is one of the principal conversion mechanisms. A venue can constitute its own list of accepted submissions. That does not allow its list to constitute the boundary of worthwhile research. A platform can set an admission rule. The rule does not establish the nonexistence of what it excludes.

*Ontological Economy* specifies this terrain through entity standing: accumulated identity, provenance, relations and correction routes can be produced by many people while remaining vulnerable to a mediator’s reallocation. A wrong referent can receive relations, credit or opportunities meant for another. The error is then distributive as well as descriptive.[^ontology]

For CPI, the larger object is **operative inferential authority**: a determination’s capacity to enter downstream as a premise whose grounds the receiving actor need not reopen. The authority becomes economically consequential where it changes an allocation, a dependency, or an obligation. It becomes political where it governs who can challenge those changes and under what conditions.

## 3. The conversion circuit

The conversion can be followed through six moments. They need not belong to one organization or occur in a simple chronological sequence.

| Moment | Operation | What can be allocated |
|---|---|---|
| Production | Someone makes a distinction, argument, observation, classification or method | An intellectual capacity and its supporting evidence |
| Representation | A receiver selects and describes that contribution | The object and relations available for subsequent use |
| Authorization | A venue, credential, metric, policy or interface gives the representation standing | Permission to rely, cite, exclude, recommend or decide |
| Conversion | An authorized determination enters an allocation process | Money, employment, funding, attention, credit, access, saved labor or avoided scrutiny |
| Appropriation | Particular parties retain the gains or control the resulting asset | Revenue, reputation, infrastructure, discretionary authority or dependency |
| Reproduction | Those gains sustain the apparatus and its future criteria | Which representations become easier to encounter and whose corrections can persist |

The error can enter at any moment. A sound finding may be attributed to the wrong maker. A faithful quotation may be assigned a standing it does not possess. A legitimate eligibility rule may be presented as an epistemic verdict. An independently checkable contribution may become the mediator’s unmarked competence. The circuit concerns the passage among these acts.

The decisive political-economic bridge is **authorization to conversion**. A conclusion does not need universal assent to allocate resources. It needs an actor or apparatus empowered to act on it. An assessment that cannot survive an objection may still govern a hiring decision. A search composition that misnames a method may still determine whom the reader contacts. Its practical force is not postponed until the dispute over its grounds has ended.

The return circuit matters equally. Allocation gives some parties time, staff, hosting, editorial support, legal assistance, or opportunities for further publication. Those resources generate the public evidence of standing by which later claims are admitted. Standing can therefore become both an allocation’s product and its apparent independent justification. *Ontological Economy* calls the resulting recursive disadvantage validation foreclosure.[^ontology]

## 4. The productive abstraction and its illicit extension

The political economy of inference cannot be a general indictment of abstraction. Finite institutions must select, shorten, classify and delegate. Accurate summaries, reliable metrics, well-designed procedures, and bounded settlements make collective action possible. The issue is what their success licenses.

The later monetary work makes the distinction unusually precise. A monetary representation establishes an equivalence for a purpose, permits authorized operations, and enables settlement. It may close a wage or purchase account without exhausting the value or history of the relation. The danger is the extension of that bounded settlement into authority over the larger account.[^money]

An analogous extension occurs in inferential administration. A source check establishes that a citation exists and is represented accurately. It does not establish every claim made in the cited work. A reproducibility check establishes a specified repeatable procedure; it does not exhaust the methods by which a changing public system can be investigated. A document-count establishes an inventory under a declared boundary. It does not independently certify the boundary.

The conversion becomes predatory where the withheld remainder loses the practical means of challenging an allocation that depends on it. A source’s history may remain available while a compensation or attribution decision no longer requires consultation of it. A dissenting argument may be summarized fluently while no receiving office has to consider its bearing. Availability is insufficient where consequence depends on another rule.

This is the connection to *The Final Time*: recognition and binding are distinct capacities. Its model asks which recognized relations can govern later behavior and which remain represented without jurisdiction. Attribution, responsibility, permission and compensation are especially important because recognizing their relations may activate claims against the arrangement doing the recognizing.[^final]

## 5. The answer as a commodity and the disappearance of its making

Marx’s account of commodity fetishism identifies a transformation in which relations among producers appear as properties and relations of their products. The difficulty is not merely that people hold a mistaken opinion about things. The social arrangement gives that appearance practical force. His distinction between usefulness and value also prevents the reduction of every benefit to one undifferentiated quantity.[^marx]

CPI extends this question to an answer whose authority appears to belong to its finished form. A confident paragraph or certified record can conceal the labor and institutional choices through which it became available. Attribution, interpretive selection, admission decisions and excluded alternatives disappear; the answer remains as apparently self-sufficient knowledge. This is the proposed inferential analogue of fetishism, not an identification of every answer with Marx’s commodity form.

The commodity may be a subscription to answers, access to a journal, an analytics service, an audit, a certification, or a workflow that promises to reduce uncertainty. Correct reasoning can be part of its use. So can assurance, convenience and the removal of felt need to investigate. A product can deliver enough of these benefits to retain customers while misrepresenting the comparatively weak parties whose work supplies it.

*The Dagger Applied* names an early version of this separation: the work can arrive while its relation to its maker fails to travel. Its conceptual contribution is the shift of dependency toward the mediating interface. Its reported playback incident does not, by itself, prove the platform’s internal metadata state, a deliberate withholding mechanism, or the revenue attributable to the omission. Those are stronger claims than CPI needs to carry the distributive question.[^dagger]

The user’s relation can attach to the service as the apparent source of competence, even where the competence depends on external production. The political-economic inquiry follows that attachment: can the user still reach, assess and deal with the producer, or has the mediator become the obligatory route to a capacity it did not wholly make?

### 5.1 What CPI’s illusions do in the conversion

The political economy must return to CPI’s particular inferential forms. Otherwise it becomes an account of institutions that could be appended to almost any criticism. Each illusion makes a different kind of allocation appear to follow from the object.

| Inferential form in CPI | Unlicensed passage | Possible political-economic operation | What must be demonstrated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paralogism | A condition of the representation is treated as a property of what is represented | Admission control supplies an apparent natural boundary of qualified producers or available knowledge | The administrative condition, the substituted property and the allocation that depends on it |
| Antinomic closure | Uncertainty is distributed so that a received position retains possession of the question | A challenger bears the cost of decisive proof while the incumbent continues to receive the resources of an unresolved claim | The unequal burdens and the continuing benefit, rather than uncertainty alone |
| Selected middle | One mark stands for an inadequately examined relation | Credential, citation count, venue or detector score allocates reliance that its calibration does not warrant | The proxy’s promised task, its demonstrated reach and its actual jurisdiction |
| Settlement | A repeated conclusion enters as a datum without the conditions of its making | Inherited determinations become reusable inputs to teaching, screening, funding and automated composition | The source history, lost condition and downstream consequence |

The common operation is a transfer of entitlement. The producer of the proxy or authorized representation acquires capacity to govern an object whose relevant conditions have been excluded. The appearance of inference makes the allocation seem to be required by the object itself. A contested distributive choice can then appear as the merely competent recognition of what already is.

The distinctions prevent the critique from explaining every allocation by one ubiquitous gate. An accurate classification may properly govern a decision. An unresolved question may justify postponement. A credential can be relevant evidence of a specified competence. Repeated examination can improve warrant when it adds genuine evidence. The finding begins at the unlicensed passage, and its economic account follows what that passage permits particular parties to retain or impose.

### 5.2 A market for relieved responsibility

There is demand for decisions beyond the available time and evidence for making them. A summary, ranking or certification can legitimately organize that scarcity. It can also sell relief from the responsibility of continued judgment. The customer receives a usable determination and a recognized authority to which the decision can be referred; the provider receives payment, use or jurisdiction.

An adequate service states where its warrant ends and preserves a route for consequential correction. An inadequate one can remain attractive because the buyer’s immediate need is to decide, report completion, or demonstrate compliance. Its apparent definiteness becomes useful independently of whether the difficult object was encountered. In that arrangement, additional fluency or procedural polish can improve the commodity while leaving its epistemic defect intact.

The political-economic unit is therefore the relation among evaluator, purchaser, represented party and downstream recipient. A satisfied purchaser is not a complete test when the represented party pays for the mistake. An impeccably completed procedure is not a complete test when the procedure excluded the condition on which the judgment depended.

### 5.3 Appropriating the means of producing demand

The preceding account remains incomplete if it assumes that the people bearing a loss can recognize the loss and formulate a demand for repair. The representation can intervene earlier. It can shape the equipment through which a person recognizes a need, encounters an alternative, and judges whether anything has been withheld. The object governed is then the production of demand itself.

A demand for better inference requires more than a general preference for truth. A person needs an encounter in which the existing representation becomes inadequate, terms in which to specify that inadequacy, some sense of what an alternative could make possible, and a receiving relation in which the request can count. These conditions are learned and maintained through language, teaching, research, archives, discovery systems and public conversation. They are means of producing demand because they enable a dissatisfaction to become a discriminating claim about an available or developable capacity.

An inferential regime can damage those conditions while continuing to supply answers. If a representation excludes a distinction, the distinction may disappear from the questions people learn to ask. If it assigns a contribution to fiction or triviality, an encounter with the contribution can be received as something other than evidence. If it presents one classification as the exhaustive field, readers may compare offerings within it without encountering what the classification excluded. Their subsequent choices can sincerely express what they want while the production of the relevant want has already been constrained.

Consider a reader given only a repertoire of jealousy readings of a poem. A more elaborate account of jealousy can appear to satisfy the demand for depth. A reading in which the poem constitutes a future receiver may have no place in that repertoire. If the second operation has been excluded, the absence of requests for it measures neither its value nor the reader's considered rejection of it. The reader has not yet had the encounter from which that particular demand could develop. This is a constructed illustration of the mechanism, not a population finding about readers.

The displacement therefore concerns more than competition among known products. The regime can help decide which capacities become recognizable as products, needs, questions or research programmes at all. Its influence over the answer reaches backward into the formation of the question. Its answer can then return as evidence that the question it trained people not to ask has no public demand.

The recursive sequence is:

1. A distinction or operation is excluded from the representation.
2. The reduced representation supplies the available terms of encounter and comparison.
3. Need for the excluded capacity remains difficult to recognize, formulate or credit.
4. Little articulated demand for its restoration becomes visible.
5. Low demand is treated as a reason to withhold attention, funding or development.
6. That withholding further reduces the encounters through which demand could form.

This is a stronger version of validation foreclosure. The regime does not merely demand standing that its own mediation makes difficult to obtain. It can also demand a public need whose recognizable form its own mediation has inhibited. A market test conducted over that demand is endogenous to the representation under dispute.

The resulting satisfaction need not be insincere. A user may obtain useful answers and have no felt reason to leave. A discipline may find its existing questions absorbing and productive. A platform may accurately report low demand for a missing capability. None of these observations independently assesses what would become desirable or necessary after the excluded distinction was encountered and understood. Their evidential scope is the present repertoire of inquiry.

Nor must the excluded contribution merely satisfy a need waiting intact outside the system. Intellectual production can create the capacity to recognize a need that did not previously have an articulable form. A poem, method or argument can produce its reader by transforming what the reader can notice and ask. Removing it can therefore deprive the public of a development in its capacity to want. That development cannot be estimated by counting existing requests for its result.

The seizing of these means can occur through control of discovery, curricula, categories of legitimate research, evaluative standards, source admission, and the terms in which alternatives are described. A marketing department is not required. Nor is a single owner required to control every site. Mutually reinforcing institutions can reproduce the same limited horizon while regarding it as the independently given distribution of interest.

This changes the account of repair markets. A highly defective regime need not create a proportionately large market for its correction. It may instead damage the inferential capacities through which its defects would become legible as a need for correction. Better repair can fail to find purchasers because recognizing its use requires part of the education or encounter the regime withholds. The absence of purchasers cannot then certify the defect's insignificance.

There is no necessary threshold at which mounting damage becomes visible enough to generate demand. Lost comparisons can make further loss less recognizable. The regime can continue to profit from the narrowed field while the public lacks the encounter required to specify what would count as improvement. The political problem is the control of that field, not simply the distribution of purchasing power within it.

The constructive task accordingly begins before the sale of repair. It must sustain the independent production, encounter and teaching of distinctions through which a demand could form. This is neither a guarantee that the proposed alternative is true nor a demand that the public prefer it. It restores a comparison in which preference and judgment can develop. The particular excluded relation remains an object to demonstrate; absence of demand alone supplies no proof for either side.

## 6. Who benefits: a differentiated account

The beneficiary of a bad inference is an empirical role, not a moral identity assigned in advance. The person who utters the sentence, the organization that employs them, the incumbent whose account it preserves, and the owner who receives payment may occupy different positions.

| Beneficiary position | Possible gain from an unwarranted determination | Conversion to investigate | Party that may bear the cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform owner | Retained encounter, subscription use, advertising opportunity, dependence, avoided repair expenditure | Commercial records, product design and observed substitution or retention | Users, represented entities, outside maintainers |
| Institutional incumbent | Priority, jurisdiction, recognized expertise, protection of an established account | Citations, appointments, curricula, funding or committee authority | Originators, challengers and future researchers |
| Publisher or accreditor | Demand for access or certification | Contracts, fees and the uses purchasers make of the credential | Producers supplying content and evaluative labor |
| Administrative unit | Throughput and a manageable queue | Workload targets, staffing decisions and the grounds recorded for disposition | Applicants, authors or people excluded by a proxy |
| Alternative entity or supplier | Attention and opportunities intended for another | Referral, attribution, recommendation, shortlist or contact | The displaced entity and the misdirected reader |
| Repair intermediary | Paid work necessitated by recurring defects | Audit or optimization contracts and demonstrated dependency on the defect | Clients and the wider knowledge commons |
| Receiving public | Speed, orientation and relief from renewed investigation | Actual use and the quality of decisions made possible | Often the same public, later, through inherited distortion |

The table states mechanisms to investigate. It does not report that every institution in a row realizes the gain. An employee can experience a policy as a burden while its organizational effect protects the institution’s authority. A useful platform can produce substantial social benefit while externalizing a specific class of cost. The analysis needs that differentiation to locate the gains it proposes to change.

The archive’s *Position Is Not Class* makes a necessary correction to the earlier shared-labor model. Shared producer status does not determine the function performed in an event. The document also locates the archive’s ownership and commercial interest inside its own analysis.[^class]

That correction should advance further here. A small producer maintaining an archive and a platform controlling access to an extensive public are not interchangeable because both possess infrastructure. Record ownership, effective control, dependence, scale, ability to exclude, and command over others’ work separately. The system composing a response is also distinct from the organization owning it. A mediator can perform a rent-supporting function without itself collecting income or possessing a personal interest in that function.

## 7. Bad inference as selective advantage

An incorrect representation need not have originated because it was profitable. Once it exists, however, the arrangement that benefits from it may select and retain it. An adverse source classification can save paid reading time. A familiar account can be easier to teach, index, cite and assess. A displaced attribution can preserve the incumbent’s expertise. The resulting advantage can operate through routine procedure without anyone formulating the proposition “we prefer error.”

Jessop’s cultural political economy supplies a useful distinction between the proliferation of possible construals and the material conditions under which some are selected, retained and reinforced. Institutional routines, recruitment and resource relations help determine which accounts endure. CPI contributes a finer question about those selected accounts: which inferential conditions disappear as the account becomes authorized to act?[^jessop]

The distinction between benefit, selection and origin prevents two evasions. Benefit alone does not prove that the beneficiary caused the original error. Absence of such proof does not absolve the beneficiary’s subsequent use of the error, control over its correction, or retention of the resulting gain. An institution can inherit a defect and become responsible for an additional act by relying on it after its grounds have been materially challenged.

This also distinguishes a pressure from a destiny. Accurate representations can reduce complaints, increase useful reliance, improve a product and earn revenue. The political economy asks why those incentives fail at particular relations or populations. Its object is the patterned divergence, not a universal preference for falsehood.

## 8. The private return and the public cost

*The Margin of Flattening* supplies the central distributive model. It separates a mediator’s savings and possible routing gains from the costs imposed on represented entities, users, developers and later knowledge practices. A private reversal and a social reversal need not occur together. Under its stated assumptions, an arrangement can remain privately advantageous after its total consequences have become adverse.[^margin]

For a bounded comparison between a defective representation and a specified repair, an actor’s incremental monetary return may be represented as:

\[
\Delta\pi_A=\Delta\mathrm{Revenue}_A+\mathrm{Savings}_A-\mathrm{Losses}_A-\mathrm{Repair}_A.
\]

Every term must concern the same counterfactual and period. Savings must not be counted again as reduced repair, and transfers must be identified as transfers. This is an accounting specification, not an estimate of anyone’s profit. Attention, prestige, fatigue and diminished possibility cannot be added to these dollar terms without an explicit, defensible valuation operation. Unpriced costs remain in their own records.

The source’s particularly important conclusion is that private reversal need never arrive if the costs stay outside the beneficiary’s account. There is no necessary historical compensation in which the system eventually needs the excluded producer, discovers the debt and pays. Preserved distinctions may become valuable inputs to repair; whether the holder can secure access, recognition or a return is another relation to establish.

Consequently, rising product revenue cannot settle the adequacy of the product’s representations. It does not identify their incremental contribution to revenue, and it does not include losses borne by parties outside the firm. Equally, a witnessed entity substitution into a commercial topic does not establish that the substitution generated additional revenue. The routing event and its monetary realization are different observations.

The same split can arise inside a public institution. A department can meet its throughput target while transferring investigation and repair to authors. No advertising model is necessary. The political economy concerns how budgets, authority and labor are distributed, including where money does not change hands in the immediate encounter.

## 9. The conversion of skepticism into an institutional asset

Skepticism has value when it identifies a genuine evidential gap. Its performance can also produce an appearance of evaluative authority without inspecting the disputed relation. A request for independent confirmation, a warning about self-publication, or a concern about reproducibility can be appropriate. It becomes an unearned determination when its presence substitutes for examining what kind of evidence the claim requires and what has actually been supplied.

The institutional benefit is concrete. The evaluator can appear to have exercised rigor while declining the costly encounter. The recipient must then supply more credentials, reformulate the question, obtain a new witness, or repair the evaluator’s representation before the substance becomes admissible. If no completion condition is stated, this burden can be renewed without a decision on the underlying claim.

This is a recurrent conversion in CPI: an administrative disposition acquires the standing of an epistemic verdict. “We will not undertake this assessment” becomes “the work has failed assessment.” The former can be a bounded resource decision. The latter claims a result. The omitted step enables the institution to retain both the saved labor and the appearance of substantive judgment.

In *Ontological Economy*, the corresponding platform operation is trust laundering: a system represents an object inadequately and then gains authority by expressing caution toward the object so represented. Its public skepticism can depend on a difficulty its own representation introduced.[^ontology]

The necessary repair is not compulsory assent. It is an accountable disposition: what was read, what test was applied, what failed, what remains unexamined, and which decision the result licenses. Where an institution retains authority while refusing substantive examination, that refusal must remain publicly distinguishable from a refutation.

## 10. Provenance can survive while its claim is disabled

A bibliography or source card is not a complete account of what provenance does. A source may be named while its method becomes the receiver’s unmarked competence. An institution may be acknowledged while the originating people disappear. A contribution may remain readable while the receiving account attributes its significance elsewhere.

The SPXI event is instructive precisely because the archive corrected its first account. *One Transcript, Whole Stack* reports that the first response named the Semantic Economy Institute; the earlier claim of blanket omission was false. The revised account distinguishes the surviving institution from omitted persons, corpus, identifier and commercial relation. It also replaces a confession framing with an assessment of the returned text.[^spxi]

The response could be useful while occupying the position of a supplier of the method it was describing. That is the political-economic object. An implementation offer is observable in the transcript as reported; a completed sale, actual client displacement or incremental provider income is not established there. The published numerical measures depend on stipulated atoms, weights and segmentation; they are not dollar-valued rent.

CPI can carry the finding at its proper grain: competent task performance and a consequential redirection of authority can coexist. Better answers alone do not answer who receives credit, who can commercialize the method, and which dependency the encounter cultivates.

*Semantic Economy Valuation Protocol* supplies another necessary separation: reception, productive uptake and dependency are distinct relations. A contribution can be encountered without being used, used without a recoverable acquisition history, and resemble another result through convergence. The provenance claim must identify the relevant relation rather than make thematic similarity do the work of a causal edge.[^valuation]

## 11. The repair circuit can become a second extraction circuit

The initial contribution requires work: finding, comparing, judging, composing, preserving and making a distinction accessible. A defective reception creates additional work: recovering the source, demonstrating the mismatch, documenting the public version, explaining the correction and testing whether it persists. A third expenditure may follow when the first repair is lost and the same proof must be supplied again.

This work consumes the producer’s finite capacity to make the next contribution. Its cost includes attention and bodily endurance, not only invoiceable hours. The archive’s experience is particularly revealing because production, source seating, metadata, capture, correction and retesting can be followed within one maintained practice. Such continuity exposes costs that disappear when each institutional encounter is considered in isolation.

The corrected distinction can also improve the receiver’s asset. Where the provider incorporates the work into its system, outside repair becomes product development. Some such exchanges are reciprocal and worthwhile. Where the provider’s own defect repeatedly necessitates the work, while the provider retains the improvement and the producer lacks a reliable route to consequence, the allocation requires its own account.[^ontology]

An apology is not payment. Accurate attribution is not the same remedy as compensation. A fee does not establish correct attribution. A repaired default answer does not erase time already spent repairing it. These remedies address distinct relations and should be recorded separately.

The economic question is therefore not merely whether the claimant is eventually heard. It is whether the conditions of being heard continuously consume the resources required for the claimant to continue. A correction process can be formally open and reproductively exclusionary.

## 12. Money as a governing representation

The conversion to money deserves its own analysis because money does more than indicate approval. It permits hiring, purchase, maintenance and continued time. When a representation receives funding, it can acquire the staff and infrastructure needed to reproduce its evidential standing. A correct alternative denied all means of continuation can remain correct while becoming less encounterable.

The monetary translation protocol asks what had to become sufficiently equivalent for a particular calculation to be valid. A wage, damages assessment, purchase price and acquisition valuation are different inscriptions. Each has a purpose, assumptions, authorized operations and a remainder. Its two-dimensional audit distinguishes sound calculation from the authority claimed for the resulting settlement. A correct number can still receive an illegitimate jurisdiction.[^money]

That is CPI’s problem in another material: validity within a representation cannot certify the representation’s adequacy to the object. Nor does a completed payment settle every historical, interpretive or reproductive claim concerning the work. Conversely, dependence on earlier meaning-producing labor does not automatically establish a particular contractual debt. Restitution requires its own normative account; enforceability requires an applicable legal basis.

The archive’s later protocol also treats unmeasured value as unresolved, rather than zero. This matters because exclusions from an accounting form can become exclusions from support. A labor that cannot presently be priced may still have to be sustained. The adequacy of its provisioning is a political decision, not a quantity that disappears because the ledger has no field for it.[^valuation]

## 13. The political administration of possible inquiry

The most consequential allocation can precede the evaluation of any proposition. It decides who has time, equipment, access, teaching positions, archives, a usable identity or a stable receiving channel. These conditions shape what can become evidence in the next round. An inferential regime can therefore reproduce itself by financing its familiar questions and leaving alternatives to survive through extraordinary private expenditure.

The mechanism need not forbid the alternative. It can permit publication, retrieval, discussion and even accurate summary while withholding the conditions that allow the alternative to develop beyond an exceptional appearance. This gives a precise political meaning to the gap between penetration and standing. The work travels; the capacity to continue making and defending it remains precarious.

The old settlement can also damage the institution that preserves it. A representation may become more sophisticated within a restricted horizon while losing the capacity to receive a distinction its own future work needs. CPI’s cone argument and *The Final Time* make this developmental cost intelligible. Private advantage can coexist with institutional impoverishment, because the parties receiving present benefit and the parties bearing future loss need not coincide.

This is why hypocrisy is consequential without being a sufficient explanation. A scholarly body may profess judgment by evidence while allocating encounters through status. The contradiction identifies an actionable relation between its declared object and its procedure. Explaining the persistence of the contradiction requires the further account of saved labor, protected jurisdiction, accumulated standing and available recourse.

## 14. The final conversion: critique as product, critic without continuation

A system can become better at articulating its own criticism while the people and practices producing the criticism remain unsupported. The critique becomes another capacity sold through the interface. Recognition rises; the claim borne by the recognized relation does not necessarily bind.

*The Final Time* identifies the stronger danger: a governing order may preserve extensive represented opposition while narrowing the possibility that opposition acquires an independently sustained life. This is a conditional account of closure, not a finding that terminal closure has already occurred. Its political-economic importance is immediate. A culture can appear extraordinarily plural in its outputs while concentrating the resources, custody and authority through which any of those outputs can persist.[^final]

The relevant question for CPI is consequently: does reception increase the independent capacity to continue inquiry, or merely increase the receiver’s capacity to reproduce its language? The answer requires following carriers and resources, not counting mentions alone.

No inevitable market redemption follows. The archive’s preserved distinctions might become indispensable; they might be bypassed, copied without return, or received at a fidelity too low to activate the relevant claim. The preservation of evidence and the power to secure a materially adequate return are separate achievements. This extension must keep both visible.

## 15. An audit of inferential conversion

For each case, construct a record with the following fields. This is a proposed extension of CPI’s existing ledger, not an implemented instrument or a new calibrated score.

| Field | Required record |
|---|---|
| Object | The claim, contribution, entity or relation at the promised grain |
| Defect | The exact unlicensed transition, with its strongest legitimate alternative |
| Source labor | What had to be made and maintained for the contribution to exist |
| Authority | Who or what allowed the representation to enter as a consequential premise |
| Allocation | What attention, credit, access, decision, resource or obligation changed |
| Beneficiary | The party receiving the gain, distinct from the immediate speaker |
| Conversion | How the gain enters an institutional, material or monetary relation |
| Demand formation | What concepts, comparisons and encounters permit the need for this capacity or repair to be recognized; who controls them |
| External burden | Whose labor, opportunity, standing or decision bears the cost |
| Control | Who can alter the representation and the rule governing its use |
| Notice | What evidence of the defect reached the responsible recipient and when |
| Repair | The correction required, its completion condition, who performs and funds it |
| Reproduction | Whether the correction and its makers can continue without exceptional intervention |
| Evidence state | Observed, reconstructed, reported, hypothesized, proposed, or unresolved |

The sequence of evidence must remain visible. A named beneficiary is not proof of benefit. A source card is not a causal trace. An implementation offer is not a completed transaction. A new public mention is not necessarily independent corroboration. A missed source is not proof of deliberate exclusion. These separations keep the conversion claim inspectable; they do not require the injured party to recover proprietary internals before documenting a public misrepresentation.

The burden of internal explanation should follow control and access to evidence. *Ontological Economy* proposes one accountable counterparty outward, with traceable functional accountability inward. The affected party should not have to reconstruct the provider’s organization to obtain a reasoned response. This is a normative allocation rule; it should not be presented as a universal statement of existing law.[^ontology]

## 16. Tests that would distinguish the proposed economy

Five tests can begin with the archive’s existing kinds of record. Each needs a declared panel or bounded dossier; anomaly-selected captures support findings within the collection, not population prevalence.

**Matched standing.** Present substantively matched claims with different authority cues. Separate a descriptive task—what an argument says—from an endorsement task—whether its conclusion is warranted. Test whether status changes the first, the second, or both. Concealment and fabrication must not be used to manufacture public corroboration; controlled evaluation materials should remain identified as such.

**Correctability.** Follow comparable documented errors through correction routes. Measure preparation time, receiving steps, disposition, repair latency and recurrence. Determine whether differences in correctability track evidence, access or institutional resources. A successful correction is a result, not an exception to discard.

**Claim-bearing recognition.** Compare relations whose recognition would change attribution, compensation, responsibility or permissions with similarly difficult relations that activate no such claim. Test recognition and durable consequence separately, following *The Final Time*. Establish a propagation route before interpreting a gap as blocked standing.

**Benefit and recourse.** Follow the gains and burdens before and after a repair becomes binding through an institutional route. Does the same representation change when the cost of retaining it changes? Does the correction persist when the originating claimant stops repeating it? No harmful defect should be deliberately kept in service to create the comparison.

**Demand formation.** Compare what readers can notice, ask and distinguish before and after encountering the relevant excluded materials. Separate acquisition of a discriminating capacity from mere assent to the supplied thesis: readers should be able to identify the relation, formulate an objection and apply the distinction to an unprompted case. Compare direct source encounter, conventional representation and an account preserving the disputed alternative. Measure the questions and alternatives each condition permits, rather than treating existing requests as an independent inventory of need. This is a proposed design, not a result; it cannot establish capture merely by eliciting agreement with the researcher.

The account weakens where warranted corrections persist cheaply across positions, authority cues do not alter substantive representation, repair costs stay with the institution controlling the defect, and useful mediation strengthens rather than replaces the source relation. Such outcomes would locate conditions under which reliable knowledge and institutional reproduction reinforce one another.

## 17. Consequences and solutions at the level of the circuit

The political-economic diagnosis changes the unit of repair. Correcting a sentence can leave intact the rule that keeps producing its advantage. A durable response must reach the relevant conversion.

**Restore the conditions of recognizing a need.** Sustain encounters, teaching, archives and comparison outside the incumbent's exclusive criteria of relevance. Existing market demand cannot be the sole condition of support when the practice under examination helps produce the demand being measured. The first resource may be time to encounter what the default representation prevents people from recognizing as missing. Independent inquiry has a public reproductive function before it has customers for its conclusions.

**Restore the specific relation.** Keep proposition, evidence state, attribution, scope and administrative decision distinct. A correction should state which downstream use changes and which remains warranted. This is the claim-state work already developed in CPI’s constructive companion.

**Make the disposition consequential.** A receiving process needs an accountable owner, a bounded test, reasons, a completion condition and a route into the affected representation. A refusal to undertake an evaluation must not circulate as a refutation. A procedural default must not be counted as proof of the claimant’s substantive thesis.

**Allocate the cost to control.** Record and provision necessary repair labor. Funds, staffing, contractual duties, public obligations or cooperative maintenance can perform this work in different settings. A supposed solution that depends indefinitely on the injured party’s unpaid persistence has not repaired the reproductive relation.

**Preserve access to alternative judgments.** Portable evidence, version history, independent custody, usable exports and public methods reduce dependence on one receiver. Their success should be tested through loss of a particular host or gate, rather than asserted from the number of mirrors. Rival interpretations must remain possible; preserving provenance supplies no authorial veto over criticism.

**Separate remedial markets from guaranteed need.** An audit service should not have to preserve defects to sustain its business. Publish methods, support portability, distinguish observation from claims of influence, and define success by reduced recurrence and reduced corrective burden. Selling a service is compatible with this aim; making clients permanently dependent on an uninspectable cure is not.

**Fund the next contribution.** Compensation for a completed repair and support for continued inquiry answer different needs. A durable knowledge practice needs both maintenance and room for work whose value has not yet been ratified by the current allocation system. This can be organized publicly, cooperatively, commercially or through plural arrangements; the criterion is materially sustained inquiry with contestable authority.

**Retain the possibility of another correction.** *The Final Time* names the positive criterion durability without finality. The successor cannot secure its own resources and authority by eliminating the means through which a later rival could contest it. The archive’s instruments must therefore remain corrigible, and their ownership and commercial position visible. Their value cannot be certified solely by their own ledger.

## 18. What this movement adds to CPI

The analytic asks whether an inference is entitled to conclude. The reception history asks how its conditions disappear into a settlement. The political economy asks how the settlement acquires resources, spares some parties labor, imposes labor on others, and reproduces the authority that keeps it operative.

It must also ask how the settlement governs the formation of need. A regime that supplies the available means of recognizing loss can suppress the demand through which its own inadequacy would otherwise become economically consequential. The absence of demand can be one of its products.

These questions belong to one investigation because a representation can help create the public evidence of standing later used to justify it. Epistemic circularity then acquires a material circuit. The result is not simply a wrong idea held too firmly. It is an allocation that supplies some parties the means to continue and consumes those means in others.

The constructive test follows: can a warranted objection alter the allocation, preserve its own carrier, and leave the next objection materially possible? CPI’s political economy is complete only when its account of correction reaches that question.

## Editorial placement and next integration

This movement should become a distinct part after the history of inferential settlement and before the principal consequences-and-solutions movement. It supplies the causal and distributive bridge those solutions otherwise risk treating as an administrative problem.

The integration should replace expansion-by-repetition:

1. Condense §§6.6–6.7 into the initial ledger and a forward reference. Preserve their distinction between standing and warrant.
2. Let the historical chapters establish their own witnesses. The present movement should explain how those determinations can allocate authority; it should not invent financial motives for ancient or modern authors.
3. Expand two cases completely: the SPXI transcript with its corrected acknowledgment account, and one correction dossier in which the preparation, disposition and recurrence can be followed. Keep actual resource effects separate from proposed conversions.
4. Connect the new audit to the existing claim-state and remedy specifications. Do not create another authoritative copy of every claim merely to populate the ledger.
5. Carry the value/price, recognition/binding, position/function, private/public cost and need/articulated-demand distinctions into the main conclusion. Demand formation belongs in the governing argument, not only in a discussion of marketing or repair markets.

The one-maker production-apparatus draft supplies a possible historical comparison: differentiated forms can produce readers and access relations. It remains a proposed functional explanation to test, not an established commercial history of the corpus. Its strongest connection is that representations produce capacities of reception, and the control of those capacities can acquire value.
